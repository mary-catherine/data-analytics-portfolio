# Coca-Cola Stock Market Analysis

[← Portfolio home](../../README.md)

## Business question

What patterns can be explored in historical closing prices, trading volume, moving averages, and volatility?

## Project overview

The Power BI report presents a historical price trend, moving-average comparisons, trading volume, a volatility chart and a price-movement breakdown. It includes a date filter for exploring the available observations. The report labels its displayed period as 2015–2022.

[**Download the Power BI report**](Coca_Cola_Stock_Market_Analysis.pbix)

## Analytical approach

- Explore the direction and variation of recorded closing prices over the available period.
- Compare closing prices with **50- and 200-observation** moving averages. Observations should not automatically be described as consecutive trading days when the source has missing dates.
- Review volume and the volatility visual alongside the price trend; use the movement breakdown to examine observed changes.

## Data and interpretation limits

A separate source CSV and data dictionary were not supplied for this project. The report itself notes that trading dates are incomplete. As a result, the figures, moving-average calculations, volatility method, and exact date coverage have **not been independently verified** during portfolio assembly. Open the report in Power BI Desktop to inspect it; refreshing may require restoring its original data source. No separate SQL script, Excel workbook, or Python notebook was supplied for this project.

This is a historical analysis demonstration, not investment advice. Any price-only change excludes dividends and is not total investment return.
